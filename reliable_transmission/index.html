Framing

Large messages are divided into smaller, fixed-size frames. Each frame is sent independently so a single error only requires retransmitting that frame, not the entire message.

🔐

CRC-8 Error Detection

Each frame carries a CRC-8 checksum computed by polynomial division. The receiver re-computes the CRC; a mismatch means corruption was detected.

📨

ACK / NACK

ACK confirms a frame arrived correctly. NACK signals an error and requests retransmission of the failed frame.

🪟

Sliding Window

Instead of waiting for one ACK before sending the next frame, the sender keeps a window of frames in-flight simultaneously, improving throughput.

🔄

Go-Back-N ARQ

On receiving a NACK, the sender goes back to the failed frame and retransmits it (and subsequent frames). Simple and robust error recovery.

⚡

Channel Corruption

Real networks introduce random bit errors. The channel simulator randomly flips characters in frames to mimic a noisy hospital wireless network.
